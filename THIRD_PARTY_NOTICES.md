# Third-party and recognition-method notes

Champion Coach's screenshot-recognition PoC is independently implemented for this repository.

The Team Preview safe-zone mapping strategy and public ROI measurements were cross-checked against:
- crazylei12/Pokemon-Champions-dmg_cal (MIT License, copyright 2026 crazylei12)
- https://github.com/crazylei12/Pokemon-Champions-dmg_cal

No private screenshots, labeled corpora, downloaded source images, or the project's compiled team-preview template pack are copied into Champion Coach.

Reference thumbnails for the PoC are fetched on the user's device from the public PokeAPI sprites repository and converted locally into small numeric fingerprints. The source images are not stored in this repository. Pokémon names, characters, artwork, and related trademarks remain the property of their respective rights holders.

SebNotFound/champions-calc (AGPL-3.0-or-later) was reviewed as prior art for browser-side Team Preview recognition. Champion Coach does not copy its source code or its recognition fingerprint dataset.
